# Sprint Planning

Sprint Planning is the event that kicks off the Sprint. During this meeting, the Scrum Team collaboratively defines the work to be done in the upcoming Sprint.

## Purpose

- Define Why the Sprint is valuable via a Sprint Goal (commitment for the Sprint Backlog)  
- Select What Product Backlog items can be Done toward the Sprint Goal and Product Goal  
- Define How the work will be delivered  

## Participants

- The full Scrum Team: Product Owner, Scrum Master, and Developers  

## Process — Why / What / How (Scrum Guide 2020)

1. **Why:** The Product Owner proposes why the Sprint is valuable and a Sprint Goal is collaboratively defined.  
2. **What:** The Developers select Product Backlog items that will move toward the Sprint Goal and Product Goal.  
3. **How:** The Developers create a plan in the Sprint Backlog for delivering the selected work.  

## Timebox

- Typically 2 hours per week of Sprint duration (e.g., 4 hours for a 2-week Sprint).

## Best Practices

- Ensure clear communication of priorities  
- Encourage team discussion and commitment  
- Keep the focus on achievable goals
