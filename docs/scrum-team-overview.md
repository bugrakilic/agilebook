# Scrum Team Overview

The Scrum Team is a small, cohesive unit focused on one product and one Product Goal. Per Scrum Guide 2020, there is no separate team within a team — there is one Scrum Team with three accountabilities: Product Owner, Scrum Master, and Developers.

## The Three Accountabilities

- **Product Owner**: Maximizes product value and manages the Product Backlog. Accountable for the Product Goal.  
- **Scrum Master**: Accountable for establishing Scrum and helping the team and organization be effective. A true leader who serves.  
- **Developers**: Professionals who do the work of delivering a usable Increment each Sprint that meets the Definition of Done.

## Key Characteristics (Scrum Guide 2020)

- Typically 10 or fewer people in total. No sub-teams.  
- Cross-functional, with all skills needed to deliver value without depending on others outside the team.  
- Self-managing — decides internally who does what, how, and what to work on to reach the Sprint Goal.  
- Collaborative, with collective accountability for delivering value toward the Product Goal.

## Benefits of the Scrum Team Structure

- Encourages ownership and accountability.  
- Improves communication and coordination.  
- Enhances flexibility and adaptability during development.  
- Drives continuous improvement through regular inspection and adaptation.
